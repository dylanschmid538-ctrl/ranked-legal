# Support · Calisthenics Skills – Ranked

## Get in touch

When the feedback form is available in Ranked, open **Settings → Feedback to Dylan** to send a
message or ask for help. You can send it without contact details or an iCloud sign-in. If you want
a reply, you can separately agree to provide an email address or phone number; we reply outside
the app. The message goes through Cloudflare to Apple CloudKit. Please do not include passwords
or private health details.

For privacy requests, including access to or deletion of a message you sent, email
**dylan.schmid538@gmail.com**. You can also use this address for support.

When something is wrong with a purchase, tell us what the app shows, roughly when you
subscribed, and which steps you tried. Please do not send your Apple Account password or payment details.

## Common questions

### My rank did not move after a session

A rank moves when the sets you log clear a stage you had not cleared before. Repeating
work you already own keeps you where you are — that is deliberate. The ladder measures
what you can do, not how often you did it.

### I claimed a skill I cannot actually do

Open the skill and change the claim. The rank recalculates immediately. Nothing is
locked in.

### I paid, but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap **Restore purchases** on the paywall. Check that the device uses the
same Apple Account that made the App Store purchase. Ranked has no in-app login. If access
is still missing, contact us with the message shown by the app.

### How do I cancel?

Open **Ranked Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription** for subscription help through
RevenueCat Customer Center. The separate **Manage Subscription** link with the Apple logo at the bottom of the support screen
opens Apple subscription management directly, including if the support screen cannot load. You can also use
**iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ranked → Cancel**. Apple handles cancellation;
opening management or selecting a survey answer does not cancel your subscription. Your access
continues until the end of the paid period.

### Refunds

Apple decides whether to approve refunds. Customer Center can offer a refund-request path;
completing a request does not mean it has been approved. You can also go to
**reportaproblem.apple.com**, sign in, and choose the purchase. Contact us if something in
the app caused the problem.

### Delete my training data

Open **Settings → Data & privacy → Delete local training data** and confirm. This removes your local
training history, progress, plans and body profile. You can also delete the app to
remove its local data. Your App Store subscription stays active until you cancel it in
your Apple Account.

### Can I sign in or transfer my training history?

Ranked currently has no account or in-app sign-in. Its training data stays on your device
and may be included in your device backup. Restoring a purchase restores subscription access,
not training history.
