## Common questions

### My rank did not move after a session

A rank moves when the sets you log clear a stage you had not cleared before. Repeating
work you already own keeps you where you are — that is deliberate. The ladder measures
what you can do, not how often you did it.

### I claimed a skill I cannot actually do

Open the skill and change the claim. The rank recalculates immediately. Nothing is
locked in.

### I paid, but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap **Restore purchases** on the paywall or in Settings. If it still asks, sign in with
the same Apple or Google account you used when you subscribed — a subscription belongs
to the account that bought it.

### How do I cancel?

Cancelling is done in your Apple account, not in the app: **Settings → your name →
Subscriptions → Ranked → Cancel**. Your access runs to the end of the period you paid
for. We cannot cancel it for you, and Apple does not let us.

### Refunds

Apple handles all refunds. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com**, sign in, and pick the
purchase. Write to us as well if something in the app is the reason — we would rather
fix it.

### Delete my account

**Settings → Delete account.** It removes your account and its data and revokes the Sign
in with Apple connection. It cannot be undone.

### Sign in with Apple or Google fails

Check that you have a connection, then try the other provider. If both fail twice, the
account screen offers to write a support message with the technical details already
filled in — send it and we will look.
