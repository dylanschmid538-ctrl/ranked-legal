## 1. What Ranked is

Ranked is a calisthenics training app. It rates your bodyweight strength on a nine-tier ladder,
tracks your progress through skill progressions, and shows you where each region of your body
stands. Everything it knows about your training lives on your phone — there is no account, no
server-side profile and no comparison with other users.

**Ranked is not a coach, a physiotherapist or a doctor**, and nothing in it is medical advice.
Calisthenics carries a real risk of injury. Skills like the planche, the front lever and the human
flag load joints and connective tissue heavily. You train at your own risk, you are responsible for
judging whether an exercise is safe for you, and you should get medical clearance if you have any
reason to doubt it. If something hurts, stop.

because of your training.** We will say so in the app when that happens.

Ranked's ratings are our own. They are not certified, not standardised, and carry no meaning
outside this app.
